Handwritten character model
===========================

Source: https://huggingface.co/hermitkk/alphabet-classifier
File: outputs/exports/alphanumeric_model.onnx
Model card license: MIT

The model is an EMNIST-based alphanumeric classifier. It is executed locally
inside the browser; captured form images are not sent to the model publisher.
